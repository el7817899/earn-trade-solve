Grade 8 Mathematics

Round Table Cooperative Learning Challenge

Work together, take turns, and solve real-life financial problems.

Learning Competency

Solve financial problems involving:
  1. earning money;
  2. profit and loss;
  3. buying amounts of products that represent the best value ("best buys"); and
  4. buying on terms (installment plan).

How to Play: Round Table

  1. Form a group with 5 to 10 students.
  2. Choose the number of participating members below.
  3. Each student enters their name on their assigned problem card.
  4. Students take turns solving their assigned problems and sharing their answers with the group.
  5. Discuss your solutions and help one another understand the computations.
  6. Check your answers, review the solutions, and complete the group reflection.

Reminder: All money amounts are in Philippine pesos (₱). Enter the numerical answer requested on each card.

Group Setup

Each participating student should answer the card with the matching student number.

Four Financial Topics

A. Earning Money

Calculate wages, total earnings, and commission.

B. Profit and Loss

Compare selling price and cost price to determine profit or loss.

C. Best Buys

Compare prices and quantities to find the better value for money.

D. Installment Plans

Calculate total installment payments and additional costs.
